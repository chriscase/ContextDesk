# Deep research index

This directory preserves reviewed outputs from externally run research tasks.
The reports are inputs to engineering judgment, not product truth or release
proof. Future agents should confirm important claims against primary sources
and ContextDesk's live and hermetic evidence before changing behavior.

## Retention policy

- Preserve compact provenance, reviewed synthesis, and useful machine-readable
  artifacts.
- Do not commit credentials, private endpoints, employer documentation, raw
  live traffic, or stochastic answer dumps.
- Keep external proposals separate from accepted ContextDesk types and tests.
- Convert stable wire findings into sanitized fixtures and explicit probes.
- Record inaccessible or unverified source material honestly rather than
  reconstructing it from memory.
